About Iron Trail Welding

Solid work. Honest communication. Real welders who take pride in doing it right.

Iron Trail Welding is a mobile welding and fabrication outfit based in Gatesville, Texas. We handle everything from gate and trailer repairs to custom fabrication work.

We operate fully mobile — meaning we bring the welding rig directly to your location: pastures, driveways, shop yards, equipment lots, and more.

Every weld is done with proper prep, clean fit-up, and the right process for the job. No shortcuts. No sloppy beadwork. Just strong, clean welding that holds up to abuse.
