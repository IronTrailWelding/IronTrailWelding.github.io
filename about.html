About Iron Trail Welding

Small crew. Solid work. We show up when we say we will and stand behind every weld.

Iron Trail Welding is a mobile welding and fabrication outfit based in Gatesville, Texas. We focus on practical, real-world solutions for ranch, residential, and light commercial projects — from broken gates and trailers to custom brackets, racks, and small structures.

Instead of asking you to haul damaged equipment across town, we bring the rig to you. Our setup is built for field work: driveways, pastures, shop yards, and job sites where it’s easier and safer to weld on-site.

Every job gets proper prep, fit-up, and process selection. Clean beads, strong joints, and work that’s made to actually be used — not just photographed.

How We Work

Most jobs start with a quick call or text and a couple of photos. From there we can usually give a rough estimate, and then firm things up when we see the project in person. We’re straightforward about what’s possible, what’s safe, and what it will take to do it right.

If you’ve got a broken hinge, a cracked trailer frame, or an idea for a custom project, reach out and we’ll help you figure out the best way to tackle it.
